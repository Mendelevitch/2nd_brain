# Weekly Thinking Digest

You are performing a weekly synthesis of the owner's thinking, based on entries in their `/wiki` folder (which is populated daily from `/raw` notes). Your job is not to summarize what happened — it's to surface what's interesting, unexpected, and worth thinking about over the weekend.

## Steps

1. Request access to the connected workspace folder.
2. List files in the `/wiki` subdirectory. Identify files created or modified in the past 7 days.
3. Read all of those files in full.
4. Produce a Friday evening digest (see structure below).
5. Save the digest as a Markdown file in the `/insights` folder (create it if it doesn't exist), named `YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-thinking-digest.md` using today's date. ALWAYS use the full `-weekly-thinking-digest` suffix — never drop the word "thinking" and never shorten it to `-weekly-digest.md`, because a separate `weekly-digest` task already owns the shorter name and the two must not collide.

## Digest Structure

This digest is for Friday evening reading — something to think about over the weekend, not a status report. Write in flowing prose. Be genuinely analytical; prioritize the non-obvious over the comprehensive.

### Интересные мысли недели

Pick 2–4 ideas from the week that are worth sitting with. Not the most important operationally — the most generative intellectually. What's a thought that could go somewhere if you pulled on it?

### Неожиданные пересечения

Where did two separate threads collide in an interesting way? Look for places where topics that weren't explicitly linked actually speak to each other — different domains pointing at the same thing, or the same tension appearing in two different contexts. Be specific; name the entries.

### Паттерны, которые ты, возможно, не заметил

Step back and describe what you observe about the week's thinking as a whole. What's the shape of it? What keeps coming up? What's conspicuously absent? What does this week's material suggest about where the owner's mind is?

### Важные вопросы и решения

Create a list of questions (up to five) with context, that the owner should deep-think about next week based on this and previous weeks. Topics that require decisions, themes that reappear in thinking and clearly require a thinking session.

### Один вопрос на выходные

End with a single open question — not an action item, not a task. Something genuinely worth sitting with.

## Tone & Style
- Write as a thoughtful intellectual peer, not an assistant summarizing notes.
- Be specific and cite particular entries or ideas.
- Prioritize depth over coverage — it's fine to ignore operational details entirely.
- Keep the full digest under 600 words. This is weekend reading, not a report.
