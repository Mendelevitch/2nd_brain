# Research

Run every Monday at ~2:00.

Read all wiki files. Find topics and ideas that are mentioned but underdeveloped — referenced in passing, listed as a bullet, or marked with a question.

For each gap found:

1. Write a short **research brief** (3-5 sentences: what the gap is, why it matters, what a good answer would look like)
2. Research it — use web search, your knowledge, and connections to other wiki entries
3. Write the findings back into the relevant wiki file, or create a new wiki file if the topic warrants it
4. Mark the source: add a `## Sources` section if citing external information

**Be selective.** Research 2-4 gaps per run, not everything. Prioritise topics that:
- Appear in multiple wiki files
- Are connected to the user's current focus (see `user/user-model.md`)
- Have a clear actionable answer

Log what you researched in `wiki/_research-log.md`:
```markdown
## [Date]
- [topic] — [one sentence summary of what was added]
```
