# Weekly Thinking Digest

Run every Friday at ~12:00.

Read all wiki files added or modified this week. Identify:

1. **Interesting ideas** — concepts worth developing further
2. **Unexpected intersections** — two unrelated wiki entries that connect in a non-obvious way
3. **Emerging patterns** — themes that appear across multiple entries this week
4. **Open questions** — things that were captured but never resolved or developed

Write a digest to `insights/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-digest.md`:

```markdown
# Digest — [Date]

## What came in this week
[Brief summary of volume and themes — 2-3 sentences]

## Interesting ideas
[2-4 ideas worth developing, with why they're interesting]

## Unexpected intersections
[1-3 connections between seemingly unrelated entries]

## Emerging patterns
[Themes that appeared more than once]

## One question for the weekend
[A single open question that would be worth sitting with]
```

Keep it concise. The digest should be readable in 3 minutes.
